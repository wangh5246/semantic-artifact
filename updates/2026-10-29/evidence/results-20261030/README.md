# Evidence for the 30 October results

- `e1-pairs.csv` selects numeric timing and memory fields from five pairs and excludes witness data, identities, secret randomness and machine paths. `summary.json` reports median/ranges for fixed but different business fixtures. fullProve includes witness generation, RSS is the prove process lifetime peak, and internal verify is not the later fresh-process verify.
- `summary.json` records the Full/T pair phases, client-generation-through-collection window and total session. Collection is nested in the window. These are single observations with different trust boundaries, not an equal-guarantee speedup. T retains genesis proofs and replaces transition proofs and moments with a trusted generator.
- `reason-counts-by-date.csv` holds mutually exclusive actor-day reasons, while `four-corpus-pooled.csv` sums the three observed-actor frames for each corpus. Dates and centers differ. GeoLife and Rome dates were selected by distinct actor coverage; T-Drive and Porto dates were fixed without a documented coverage-ranking rule. Rome is a cleaned raw-point cache, not the original full corpus.
- `roster-summary.csv` contains aggregate counts only. GeoLife has 15 evaluable N8 rosters and 30 insufficient N32/N100 pools. Rome has 0/11 and 0/2 common starts conditional on all members being individually legal, and N100 is N/A. Reused actors make roster outcomes dependent.
- The source SHA-256 entries in `summary.json` identify locally retained input files. Their basenames and numeric suffixes distinguish sources without exporting local paths. These hashes are provenance pointers, not assertions that private sources are included.
