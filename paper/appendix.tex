\label{thm:main-payload-fidelity}
\label{lem:submission-dpf-recurrence}
Sound certification, complete canonical key binding, correct recipient
decryption, signatures and matching DPF generator and evaluator semantics
bind an accepted payload to \(b_t\mathbf e_{\mathsf{Grid}(\mathbf x_t)}\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}[Proof outline]
The Poseidon correction recurrence~\cite{Grassi2021Poseidon} equalizes
off-target DPF states and preserves opposite target controls. The final
correction cancels off-target leaves and gives \(b_t\) at the target.
Complete key openings connect the consumed key to that certified recurrence.
\end{proof}

\(Q_\pi a_{\rm KS}+Q_Ca_{\rm Poseidon}+\alpha_{\rm dense}\).
Its reduced-field hashes and complete-key relation belong to that
historical construction, separately from the native bounds below.
